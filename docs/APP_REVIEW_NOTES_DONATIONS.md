# App Review notes — donations & guest access

Copy into App Store Connect “Notes for Review” (edit recipient wording once legal details are configured):

---

General spiritual guidance (home, puja/ritual catalogue, deity information, festival and lunar guidance, and related Know More content) can be browsed without creating an account. Sign-in is required only for account-specific actions such as beginning a guided puja/ritual, favourites, history, notifications, checkout, and personal profile data.

The Donate button opens our organisation’s public website (`https://www.sathya.co.za/donate`) in the device’s external browser (Safari on iOS). Donors select an amount and complete payment on PayFast’s hosted checkout. The app does not process donations, embed a payment WebView for donations, or collect card details inside the app.

We do not claim Benevity/Candid nonprofit approval or tax deductibility in the app or on the donation page unless that status is separately verified.

This external flow is intended to address Guideline 3.2.2(iv). We welcome clarification from Apple if further adjustments are required for our recipient arrangement.
